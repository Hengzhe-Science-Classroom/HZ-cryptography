# Cryptography · Interactive

**Symmetric, asymmetric, and zero-knowledge — the math of secrets**

**Status:** Planned

Part of [Hengzhe's Science Classroom](https://hengzhe-science-classroom.github.io/hub/) · Tier 5 · Research Frontier

## What You'll Learn

- Symmetric encryption: AES, block cipher modes
- Hash functions and MACs
- Public-key encryption: RSA, ElGamal
- Digital signatures
- Zero-knowledge proofs
- Lattice-based and post-quantum cryptography

## References

- Jonathan Katz & Yehuda Lindell, *Introduction to Modern Cryptography* (3rd ed., CRC Press)
- Christof Paar & Jan Pelzl, *Understanding Cryptography* (Springer)
- Dan Boneh & Victor Shoup, *A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography* (free online)

## Features (when built)

- **Interactive visualizations** — every concept comes alive with draggable, animated demos
- **KaTeX math rendering** — rigorous notation throughout
- **Exercises with hints & solutions** — from computational to proof-based
- **Progress tracking** — saved locally in your browser
- **Zero dependencies** — just open the link and learn
